## 🛠️ Architecture & System Design

### Q: How do you choose between REST and gRPC?
**A:** For internal inter-service communication where low latency, high throughput, and strict contract typing are critical, I use **gRPC with Protobuf**. For public client-facing surfaces, mobile clients, and partner integrations, I provide standardized **RESTful APIs** secured behind the **Kong API Gateway** with JWT authentication and rate limiting.

### Q: How do you handle asynchronous decoupling?
**A:** I leverage **Apache Kafka** and **NATS JetStream** to offload heavy workloads from the synchronous request-response cycle into event-driven pub/sub topics. This architecture reduced operational latency by 25% in our production Go microservices.

### Q: How do you approach frontend performance optimization?
**A:** I focus on eliminating duplicate network waterfalls using **TanStack Query** for intelligent server-state caching, memoizing expensive calculations, lazy loading code-split chunks, and standardizing global client state with **Redux Toolkit**.
